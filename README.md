# BrightTech Solutions — Salesforce CRM & Sales Pipeline Management

## Project Overview
This project demonstrates the implementation and documentation of a Salesforce CRM solution for a sample company, BrightTech Solutions. It focuses on organizing customer information, tracking sales opportunities, managing follow-up activities, and preparing sales reports.

## Business Problem
Managing customer details and sales opportunities manually can make it difficult to track potential customers, remember follow-ups, and monitor expected revenue. A centralized CRM helps organize this information and improves visibility into the sales process.

## Project Objectives
- Manage leads, accounts, and contacts in Salesforce.
- Convert qualified leads into accounts, contacts, and opportunities.
- Track opportunities through sales stages.
- Record follow-up tasks and next steps.
- Create reports to review the sales pipeline.
- Document the configuration and business workflow.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Platform:** Salesforce CRM
- **Interface:** Salesforce Lightning Experience
- **Reporting:** Salesforce Reports
- **Documentation:** GitHub and Markdown
