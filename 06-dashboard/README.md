# Salesforce Sales Dashboard

## 1. Objective
Plan a Salesforce dashboard that summarizes sales pipeline information for BrightTech Solutions and makes important opportunity details easier to understand.

## 2. Proposed Dashboard Components

### 2.1 Opportunities by Stage
A chart showing the distribution of opportunities across sales stages.

### 2.2 Opportunity Amount by Stage
A chart comparing the recorded opportunity amounts across stages.

### 2.3 Opportunities by Owner
A chart showing the number or value of opportunities assigned to each owner.

### 2.4 Upcoming Close Dates
A report component highlighting opportunities with approaching close dates.

### 2.5 Open Opportunities
A summary of opportunities that have not yet reached a closed stage.

## 3. Data Source
The proposed components can use suitable Salesforce Opportunities reports. Each component should be tested against the available records.

## 4. Implementation Procedure
1. Create and validate the required Salesforce reports.
2. Open the Dashboards area in Salesforce.
3. Create a dashboard.
4. Add chart or metric components based on the available reports.
5. Configure component titles and display options.
6. Save and verify the dashboard.
7. Capture a screenshot and add it to the repository.

## 5. Business Value
A dashboard can help users review the pipeline, identify opportunities that need follow-up, and understand the distribution of potential deals.

## 7. Limitations
Dashboard results depend on the quality and quantity of the underlying records. A small sample dataset is suitable for demonstration but not for reliable business forecasting.
